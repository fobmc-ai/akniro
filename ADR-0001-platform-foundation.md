# ADR-0001: 织能基础平台架构

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08
- Scope: GOV-FOUNDATION-001

## Decision

Adopt a layered, edge-autonomous, contract-first platform with strict realtime/non-realtime isolation, a canonical Machine Project, Control Plane/Data Plane separation, capability/resource registries, and governed AI.

## Consequences

The platform requires more explicit contracts and validation up front, but gains safer evolution, replaceable models/drivers, offline resilience, coherent deployment, and bounded AI context.
